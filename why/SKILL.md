# Why

Say why a choice was made, and what it cost.

## Do this

1. Read `CONTEXT.md` when the repo has it.
2. When a source-control or docs tool is available, use it for the decision they asked about. Keep the summary. Leave the log in the tool.
3. State the choice and the tradeoff. Cite the entry, the commit, or the doc that shows it.
4. Short sentences. Stay on that choice.

## Don't

- Invent a decision the file, the commit, or the doc does not show.
- Trace the implementation. That is `how`.
- Paste a history dump into the reply.

## Not this

- Nothing recorded that decision. Say that.
